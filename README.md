## About Me

I am a deep-learning practitioner based in South Korea, specializing in **3D computer vision, point-cloud processing, and medical/dental AI**.

My current work focuses on building deep-learning systems for:

* 3D point-cloud completion and reconstruction
* Point-cloud classification and segmentation
* Dental crown generation and tooth segmentation
* Voxel-, point-, and hybrid representation learning
* Efficient and lightweight deep-learning architectures

I am also expanding my research into **SLAM, Neural Radiance Fields, 3D Gaussian Splatting, autonomous-driving perception, and multimodal AI**.

* 🎓 Bachelor’s degree from **Telkom University**
* 🎓 Master’s degree from **Sejong University**, South Korea — dual-major program
* 📍 Currently working in **South Korea**
* 🤝 Open to research and open-source collaboration in 3D vision, ML, and DL
* 🌐 Portfolio: [irfanfadhullah.github.io](https://irfanfadhullah.github.io/)
